# PaperLoop 0.3.40

发布文案草稿，等待审核。Browser 0.3.40 · Zotero 0.5.7.

## 中文

- 新增樱雾奶牛猫、暮杏柴犬、海盐落日三套主题，支持浅色、深色和两种插画风格。
- 缩小面板后可以变成奶牛猫或柴犬，支持拖动、眨眼、摇尾巴，点击回到笔记。
- 宠物可以跟随主题，也可以单独选择；原来的书签模式仍然可用。

## English

- Three new themes: Sakura Cat, Apricot Shiba and Sea Salt Sunset, with light/dark appearances and both illustration styles.
- Minimize into a cat or Shiba companion that blinks and wags its tail. Drag to move; click to return to your notes.
- Match the pet to your theme or choose it independently. The bookmark ribbon remains available.

Interaction inspiration: Tony Baloney and the VS Code Pets contributors; adryd325 and the oneko.js contributors. Pet artwork is newly drawn for PaperLoop; no third-party pet sprites or code are bundled. Existing editor credits remain unchanged in CREDITS.md.
